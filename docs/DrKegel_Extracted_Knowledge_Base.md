### Mind FAQ 2: What is Mind Cleaner?
Mind Cleaner is a special technique that helps to detox consciousness, relieve stress & anxiety.

### Mind FAQ 3: How does Mind Cleaner work?
Mind Cleaner uses a psychological exercise based on Freewriting. During this exercise, you have to set a timer and write without stopping for more than 5 seconds until the timer runs out. When the timer runs out, you can stop writing, reread your text, and burn it to clear your mind from the written thoughts.

### Mind FAQ 4: How often should I practice Mind Cleaner?
Try to make it a daily habit. This way it’ll be easier for you to write and increase the duration of each new session. This in turn will provide an even greater effect. Remember, you can do as many sessions a day as you want.

### Mind FAQ 5: What happens if I stop writing for more than 5 seconds?
In this case, your text will disappear and you’ll have to start the exercise all over again. This is designed to keep you from overthinking. That's why just write and don't stop. 

### Mind FAQ 6: If I write nonstop, there'll be a lot of mistakes. What about it?
It’s okay. You can make mistakes in the text. Grammar and spelling are not important here. What’s important is to get your stream of consciousness on the page.

### Mind FAQ 7: What should I write about?
You can write about things that make you feel happy or things that make you feel bad. Generally, you can write about anything that comes to your mind.

### Mind FAQ 8: What if I don't know what to write about?
If you don't know what to write about, write about how you don't know what to write about. Just start writing, even if it seems nonsense from the beginning.

### Mind FAQ 9: How to prepare for writing?
In general, this doesn’t require any special preparation. But it’s recommended to turn off the music, TV, and other distractions before you start writing.

### Mind FAQ 10: What’s the best duration to set a timer for?
You can choose any time that is comfortable for you. However, it is recommended to start with a small amount of time, such as 5 minutes, and increase the duration with each new session. Some people get to the point where they can write for more than 1 hour.
