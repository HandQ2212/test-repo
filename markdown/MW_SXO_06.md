## Demystifying the "Day 7 Abstinence Spike"

The foundational claim of the viral "NoFap" movement originates from a single study from **Zhejiang University (Jiang et al., 2003)**:
- Researchers tracked daily serum testosterone across 28 male volunteers abstaining from ejaculation.
- **Days 1 through 5:** Serum testosterone showed zero meaningful deviation from baseline.
- **On Exactly Day 7:** Testosterone sharply **spiked to 145.7% of baseline**.
- **Day 8 and Beyond:** Testosterone **immediately plunged back to normal baseline**, stabilizing there indefinitely regardless of how many weeks abstinence continued!

The scientific truth: The Day 7 spike is a brief homeostatic endocrine rebound of the HPTA axis, **not an ascending staircase**. Prolonged abstinence does not store additional androgens.

## Acute Endocrine Responses: The Exton et al. Trial

In a controlled investigation published in the **World Journal of Urology (Exton et al., 2001)**, researchers measured continuous venous blood parameters during masturbation-induced orgasm:
- **During Climax:** Marked surges in Noradrenaline, Adrenaline, and Prolactin.
- **Testosterone Levels:** Remained stable during masturbation, displayed a mild 10–20 minute elevation post-climax, and returned to resting baseline.
- Conclusion: **Ejaculation does not drain foundational androgen reserves.**
