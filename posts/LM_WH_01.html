The Four Menstrual Phases: Hormonal Drivers of Energy, Mood, and Metabolism

How the 28-day ebb and flow of Estrogen and Progesterone governs your energy, insulin sensitivity, and athletic performance.

PV
Pulse & Vitality Clinical Editorial Board

💡 Key Actionable Takeaway

Female physiology operates on a 28-day infradian rhythm; aligning your workouts and nutrition with your hormonal shifts prevents burnout and maximizes strength.

1. The Infradian Rhythm: The 28-Day Biological Clock

While male biology operates almost exclusively on a 24-hour circadian clock, female physiology is additionally governed by a 28-day Infradian Rhythm. This monthly hormonal cycle orchestrates body temperature, resting heart rate, glucose tolerance, and metabolic recovery.

2. Breakdown of the Four Hormonal Phases

  1. Menstrual Phase (Days 1–5):
    - Hormonal Profile: Both estrogen and progesterone hit rock bottom. The thickened endometrium is shed.
    - Physiological State: Energy stores are conserved. Systemic inflammation may be slightly elevated due to uterine prostaglandins. Focus on gentle movement and restorative rest.
  2. Follicular Phase (Days 6–13):
    - Hormonal Profile: The pituitary gland releases Follicle-Stimulating Hormone (FSH). Ovarian follicles mature, driving a sharp exponential rise in Estrogen (estradiol).
    - Physiological State: Estrogen increases insulin sensitivity, accelerates muscle protein synthesis, and elevates dopamine and serotonin. This is your peak window for strength gains and mental sharpness.
  3. Ovulatory Phase (Around Day 14):
    - Hormonal Profile: Estrogen peaks, triggering a surge of Luteinizing Hormone (LH) that causes the dominant follicle to rupture and release an egg. Testosterone reaches its monthly crest.
    - Physiological State: Maximal physical power, athletic stamina, and peak libido.
  4. Luteal Phase (Days 15–28):
    - Hormonal Profile: The collapsed follicle forms the corpus luteum, which secretes large quantities of Progesterone.
    - Physiological State: Progesterone raises core resting temperature by $0.3^\circ\text{C}$ to $0.5^\circ\text{C}$ and accelerates resting heart rate. Protein catabolism increases, sleep architecture becomes more fragmented, and carbohydrate cravings rise prior to menses.
