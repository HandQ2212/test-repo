Basal Body Temperature (BBT): The Thermal Signature of Ovulation

How a post-ovulatory progesterone surge elevates your resting core and skin temperature by 0.5°F (0.3°C) until the onset of menses.

PV
Pulse & Vitality Clinical Editorial Board
Clinical Wearable Reference: Natural Cycles • Mapped Screen: Menstrual Cycle Screen • Metric: Basal Body Temperature (BBT)
💡 Key Actionable Takeaway

A sustained temperature rise of 0.3°C to 0.5°C confirms that ovulation has occurred and marks the onset of the luteal phase.

1. What is Basal Body Temperature (BBT)?

Basal Body Temperature is your body's lowest resting temperature, reached during deep sleep and measured immediately upon waking (or tracked continuously overnight via wrist sensors on compatible smartwatches and rings).

2. The Bi-Phasic Temperature Shift

Your temperature curve across a natural menstrual cycle is characteristically bi-phasic, driven by the thermogenic properties of female sex hormones:
- Follicular Phase (Low Baseline): During the first half of the cycle, estrogen is dominant. Estrogen keeps core body temperature lower, typically fluctuating between 36.1°C and 36.6°C (97.0°F – 97.8°F).
- The Thermal Shift (Post-Ovulation): Immediately following ovulation, the ruptured follicle transforms into the corpus luteum, which begins secreting high concentrations of Progesterone. Progesterone acts directly on the thermoregulatory center of the hypothalamus, causing an acute sustained rise of 0.3°C to 0.5°C (0.5°F – 1.0°F).
- The Luteal Plateau: This elevated thermal plateau persists for 10 to 14 days throughout the luteal phase.
- The Pre-Menstrual Drop: If fertilization does not occur, the corpus luteum involutes, progesterone plunges, and body temperature drops back to follicular baseline on or just before Day 1 of your period.

3. Confirmation vs. Prediction

BBT tracking provides definitive physiological confirmation that ovulation has already occurred. When paired with historical cycle algorithms, it allows FDA-cleared apps like Natural Cycles to map your unique fertility window with clinical accuracy.
